## When local AI makes sense

[YES]  **You handle sensitive data** (client records, financials, legal, medical)
[YES]  **You spend $20+/month on AI subscriptions**
[YES]  **You do repetitive AI tasks** (drafting, summarizing, content generation)
[YES]  **You want no vendor lock-in** (swap models freely, no price hikes)
[YES]  **You need offline reliability** (no outages, no API rate limits)

## When cloud AI still wins

[!]  You need the absolute frontier model (GPT-5, Claude 4 Opus) for complex reasoning
[!]  You do very low volume (less than $10/month in AI costs)
[!]  You need massive context windows (1M+ tokens in a single prompt)
[!]  You generate video/images at scale (local GPU not powerful enough)

## The hybrid play (best of both)

**Run local for 80% of everyday tasks. Use cloud only for the 20% that needs frontier power.**

This cuts your AI bill by up to 90% while keeping sensitive data private and maintaining access to top-tier models when you truly need them.
